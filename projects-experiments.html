Experiment Record

Every experiment can be documented as a reproducible research record.

QUESTION

What are we trying to understand, measure or investigate?

HYPOTHESIS

What result do we expect, and why?

SETUP

What equipment, software, data or experimental conditions are required?

METHOD

What procedure or algorithm was used to perform the investigation?

OBSERVATIONS

What happened during the experiment or project?

DATA

What measurements, outputs or datasets were collected?

ANALYSIS

What patterns, relationships or results appear in the data?

CONCLUSION

What did the investigation reveal, and what should be explored next?
