How AI Works — The Big Picture

1

📊 Data

Many AI systems learn patterns from examples, which can include text, images, audio, measurements or other forms of data.

2

⚙️ Model

A mathematical model represents patterns or relationships discovered during the learning process.

3

🧪 Training

During training, the model's parameters are adjusted to reduce errors according to a chosen learning objective.

4

💡 Inference

Once trained, a model can process new inputs and produce predictions, classifications, generated content or other outputs.

Key Technology Concepts

AI

Neural Networks

Computing systems inspired loosely by biological neural networks. They contain interconnected computational units arranged in layers.

COMPUTING

Algorithms

Step-by-step procedures for solving problems or performing computations.

DATA

Big Data

Extremely large or complex datasets that require specialized methods and infrastructure to store, process and analyze.

NETWORKS

Cloud Computing

Computing resources such as servers, storage and software services delivered through networks.

SECURITY

Encryption

Mathematical techniques used to transform data so that only authorized parties can access its intended information.

ROBOTICS

Automation

Using technology to perform tasks with reduced direct human intervention.

🤖 QENOAIX Fact

Artificial intelligence is not a single technology. It is a broad field containing many approaches, including machine learning, neural networks, computer vision, natural language processing, planning and robotics.
