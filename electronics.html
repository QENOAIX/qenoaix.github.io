Electronics.

Explore the science and engineering of electronic systems, from fundamental circuits and semiconductors to sensors, microcontrollers, communication systems and intelligent devices.

What Is Electronics?

Electronics is the branch of science and engineering concerned with controlling the flow of electrical charge to process, transmit, store and use information or energy.

Electricity

Electrical charge and current provide the foundation for electronic systems.

Circuits

Electronic circuits connect components to control electrical signals and perform useful functions.

Signals

Electrical signals can represent information, measurements and commands.

How an Electronic System Works

Electronic systems commonly receive energy and information, process signals and produce a useful output.

01

Power

A power source provides the electrical energy required by the electronic circuit.

02

Input

Sensors, switches or other components provide signals or information to the system.

03

Processing

Electronic components process or transform the incoming electrical signals.

04

Control

Digital circuits or microcontrollers can make decisions based on programmed instructions and input data.

05

Output

The system produces an output through displays, motors, speakers, lights or other devices.

Electronics Pipeline

A simplified model of how information can move through an electronic system.

POWER ↓ INPUT ↓ SENSING ↓ PROCESSING ↓ CONTROL ↓ OUTPUT

Basic Electronic Components

Electronic circuits are built from components with different electrical functions.

Resistor

A resistor limits or controls the flow of electrical current and can create voltage differences.

Capacitor

A capacitor stores electrical energy in an electric field and can be used for filtering and timing.

Inductor

An inductor stores energy in a magnetic field and responds to changes in current.

Diode

A diode primarily allows current to flow in one direction and is useful in many circuit applications.

Transistor

Transistors can act as electronic switches or amplifying elements.

Integrated Circuit

Integrated circuits contain many electronic components manufactured together on a semiconductor chip.

Analog & Digital Electronics

Electronic systems can process signals in different forms, with analog and digital electronics representing two major approaches.

Analog Electronics

Analog systems process continuously varying signals such as voltage or current.

Digital Electronics

Digital systems represent information using discrete values, commonly binary states.

Mixed-Signal Systems

Many modern devices combine analog circuits with digital processing.

Semiconductors

Semiconductor materials have electrical properties that can be controlled, making them fundamental to modern electronics.

Silicon

Silicon is widely used to manufacture electronic components and integrated circuits.

Transistors

Semiconductor transistors form the basic switching elements of modern digital electronics.

Microchips

Complex electronic systems can be integrated into compact semiconductor chips.

Digital Logic

Digital circuits use logic operations to process binary information and implement computational functions.

AND

Produces a true output when all required inputs are true.

OR

Produces a true output when at least one required input is true.

NOT

Produces the opposite logical state of its input.

BINARY INPUT ↓ LOGIC GATES ↓ DIGITAL CIRCUIT ↓ PROCESSING ↓ OUTPUT

Microcontrollers & Embedded Systems

Microcontrollers combine processing, memory and input/output capabilities in compact chips designed to control electronic systems.

Microcontroller

A small computer integrated into a chip for controlling electronic devices.

Sensors

Sensors measure physical quantities such as temperature, light, motion, pressure or distance.

Actuators

Actuators convert electrical control signals into physical actions such as movement.

Sensors & Measurement

Sensors allow electronic systems to observe physical environments and convert measurements into electrical signals.

Temperature

Temperature sensors can measure changes in thermal conditions.

Light

Light sensors can detect the intensity or presence of electromagnetic radiation.

Motion

Motion sensors can detect movement, acceleration or changes in orientation.

Electronics & Communication

Electronic systems make modern communication possible by generating, processing, transmitting and receiving signals.

Radio

Radio systems use electromagnetic waves to transmit information through space.

Fiber Optics

Fiber-optic systems transmit information using pulses of light through optical fibers.

Wireless Systems

Wireless electronics allow devices to exchange information without physical communication cables.

Electronics & AI

Modern AI systems depend on electronics for sensing, computation, communication and control.

AI Hardware

Specialized processors can accelerate machine-learning computations.

Edge AI

AI models can run directly on electronic devices instead of relying entirely on remote servers.

Smart Devices

Sensors, processors and communication hardware allow electronic devices to become increasingly intelligent.

Applications of Electronics

Consumer Devices

Smartphones, computers, televisions and wearable devices rely on complex electronic systems.

Automobiles

Modern vehicles use electronics for sensing, communication, control and entertainment.

Healthcare

Electronic instruments support monitoring, measurement, imaging and medical research.

Space Technology

Spacecraft use electronics for communication, navigation, scientific instruments and control.

Robotics

Robots depend on electronic sensors, controllers, communication systems and motor control.

Industrial Systems

Electronics enable automation, measurement, monitoring and machine control.

The Future of Electronics

Electronics continues to advance toward smaller components, faster processors, lower power consumption and increasingly intelligent connected systems.

Flexible Electronics

Flexible electronic materials may enable devices that can bend or conform to different surfaces.

Neuromorphic Computing

Neuromorphic systems explore hardware architectures inspired by aspects of biological neural systems.

Advanced Semiconductors

New semiconductor technologies aim to improve performance, efficiency and specialized computing.

QENOAIX. FACT

Modern electronic devices can contain billions of microscopic transistors working together on a single integrated circuit.
