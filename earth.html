Earth.

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only world currently known to support life.

About Earth

Earth is a rocky planet with liquid water on its surface. Its atmosphere, oceans and geological activity create a complex environment capable of supporting an enormous diversity of life.

☀️ Distance from Sun

About 149.6 million km on average.

📏 Diameter

About 12,742 km.

🕐 Orbital Period

About 365.25 days.

🌍 Type

Rocky terrestrial planet.

🌊 Interesting Fact

About 71% of Earth's surface is covered by water. Earth also has a natural satellite, the Moon, which plays an important role in tides and other phenomena.
