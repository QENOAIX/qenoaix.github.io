Space Exploration.

From the first satellites and human missions to robotic explorers travelling across the Solar System, spacecraft have transformed our understanding of the universe.

What Is Space Exploration?

Space exploration is the investigation of space beyond Earth's atmosphere using spacecraft, satellites, telescopes, landers, rovers and human missions. It allows scientists to study planets, moons, asteroids, comets, stars and the wider universe.

Historic Missions

1957

🛰️ Sputnik 1

The Soviet Union's Sputnik 1 became the first artificial satellite to orbit Earth, beginning the Space Age.

1961

🚀 Vostok 1

Yuri Gagarin became the first human to travel into space during the Vostok 1 mission.

1969

🌕 Apollo 11

NASA's Apollo 11 mission carried astronauts to the Moon and achieved the first crewed lunar landing.

1977

🌌 Voyager

The Voyager spacecraft began journeys that eventually carried them into the outer Solar System and beyond.

Famous Robotic Explorers

🔴 Mars Rovers

Robotic rovers such as Spirit, Opportunity, Curiosity and Perseverance have investigated the Martian surface, geology and ancient environments.

🟠 Juno

NASA's Juno spacecraft studies Jupiter's atmosphere, magnetic field, interior and enormous polar regions.

🪐 Cassini-Huygens

Cassini spent years studying Saturn and its moons. The Huygens probe descended through the atmosphere of Saturn's moon Titan and reached its surface.

☄️ Rosetta

ESA's Rosetta mission studied comet 67P/Churyumov-Gerasimenko and deployed the Philae lander onto the comet.

Important Spacecraft

🚀 Space Shuttle

NASA's Space Shuttle system was designed for repeated launches and landings and played a major role in building and servicing the International Space Station.

🛰️ James Webb Space Telescope

The James Webb Space Telescope observes the universe primarily in infrared wavelengths, studying galaxies, stars, planetary systems and distant cosmic objects.

🏠 International Space Station

The International Space Station is a large orbital laboratory where astronauts conduct scientific research in microgravity.

🚀 Modern Crew Vehicles

Modern crew spacecraft transport astronauts to and from low Earth orbit and support the continuing development of human spaceflight.

Types of Space Missions

🌍 Earth Observation

Satellites observe Earth's atmosphere, oceans, land, weather and climate.

🪐 Planetary Exploration

Spacecraft visit planets and moons to investigate their surfaces, atmospheres, interiors and histories.

☄️ Small-Body Missions

Missions to asteroids and comets help scientists study ancient material left over from the formation of the Solar System.

🔭 Space Telescopes

Telescopes above Earth's atmosphere can observe wavelengths of light that are blocked or distorted by the atmosphere.

🚀 QENOAIX Fact

Spacecraft do not all travel in straight lines. Mission planners can use planetary gravity assists to change a spacecraft's speed or trajectory while saving propellant.
