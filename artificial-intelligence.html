Artificial Intelligence.

Explore the science and engineering behind machines that can learn from data, recognize patterns, understand language, make predictions and generate new content.

What Is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence, or AI, is a broad field of computer science concerned with building systems that can perform tasks that normally require capabilities such as perception, reasoning, learning, language processing and decision-making.

How AI Systems Work

1

📊 Collect Data

Many AI systems begin with data. Depending on the task, this could include text, images, audio, video, measurements or other information.

2

🧠 Find Patterns

Machine-learning algorithms can identify statistical patterns and relationships within training data.

3

⚙️ Train a Model

During training, a model's parameters are adjusted according to an objective so that its performance improves on the training task.

4

💡 Generate an Output

After training, the system can process new inputs and produce predictions, classifications, decisions or generated content.

Major Areas of AI

🧠

Machine Learning

Algorithms that learn useful patterns from data and use those patterns to make predictions or decisions.

👁️

Computer Vision

AI methods that allow computers to analyze and interpret images, video and other visual information.

💬

Natural Language Processing

Technologies that allow computers to process, analyze and generate human language.

✨

Generative AI

AI systems capable of generating new content such as text, images, audio, video or code.

🦾

Robotics

The combination of computation, sensing and control with physical machines that interact with the world.

🎯

Decision Systems

Systems designed to evaluate information and choose actions according to rules, learned models or optimization objectives.

Types of Machine Learning

Supervised Learning

The model learns from examples where the desired output is provided. It can then make predictions for new inputs.

Unsupervised Learning

The algorithm works with data without predefined target labels and can discover structures such as clusters or patterns.

Reinforcement Learning

An agent learns through interaction with an environment, receiving feedback in the form of rewards or penalties.

Neural Networks

Neural networks are computational models made from interconnected units arranged in layers. During training, their parameters are adjusted so that the network can represent useful patterns in data.

Input Layer

Receives the information supplied to the neural network.

Hidden Layers

Intermediate layers transform information using learned parameters and nonlinear functions.

Output Layer

Produces the final prediction, classification, probability distribution or other output.

AI in Everyday Life

📱 Smartphones

AI can assist with speech recognition, photography, recommendations, translation and other features.

🗺️ Navigation

Intelligent systems can analyze maps, traffic and other information to help estimate routes and travel times.

🎬 Recommendations

Recommendation systems analyze patterns in user activity and content to suggest potentially relevant items.

🔎 Search

Modern search systems use machine-learning techniques to understand queries and rank relevant information.

🤖 QENOAIX Fact

AI does not automatically mean a machine is conscious or thinks exactly like a human. Most AI systems are specialized tools designed to perform particular tasks using algorithms, data and computational models.
