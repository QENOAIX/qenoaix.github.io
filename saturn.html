Saturn.

Saturn is the sixth planet from the Sun and is famous for its spectacular system of rings.

About Saturn

Saturn is a gas giant composed mainly of hydrogen and helium. It is the second-largest planet in the Solar System and has a much lower average density than Earth.

☀️ Distance from Sun

About 1.43 billion km on average.

📏 Diameter

About 116,460 km.

🕐 Orbital Period

About 29.45 Earth years.

🌍 Type

Gas giant.

💍 Interesting Fact

Saturn's rings are made mostly of countless pieces of water ice mixed with rocky material. Saturn also has many moons, including Titan, a world with a thick atmosphere and lakes and seas of liquid hydrocarbons.
