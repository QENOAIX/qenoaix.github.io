Effective Study

Good studying is more than reading. Use active learning, deliberate practice and regular revision.

01

Active Recall

Close your notes and try to reproduce the idea from memory. This helps reveal what you actually know.

02

Spaced Revision

Revisit important concepts at increasing intervals instead of relying only on one long revision session.

03

Problem Solving

Practice applying concepts to different types of problems. Understanding grows when you use what you learn.

04

Error Analysis

Keep track of mistakes and identify why they happened. A mistake can become a useful learning signal.

05

Concept Connections

Connect ideas across chapters and subjects instead of treating every topic as an isolated piece of information.

06

Self Testing

Regularly test yourself under realistic conditions to measure understanding and identify weak areas.

Exam Preparation

Use a structured approach to build concepts first, then develop problem-solving speed, accuracy and exam awareness.

01. Understand the concept
02. Study solved examples
03. Practice basic problems
04. Progress to advanced problems
05. Solve previous-year questions
06. Analyze mistakes
07. Revise weak topics
08. Take timed practice tests
