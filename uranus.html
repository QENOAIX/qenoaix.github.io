Uranus.

Uranus is the seventh planet from the Sun and an ice giant known for its extreme axial tilt.

About Uranus

Uranus is an ice giant made mostly of hydrogen and helium, with significant amounts of water, methane and ammonia in its deep atmosphere and interior. Methane in the upper atmosphere contributes to its blue-green appearance.

☀️ Distance from Sun

About 2.87 billion km on average.

📏 Diameter

About 50,724 km.

🕐 Orbital Period

About 84 Earth years.

🌍 Type

Ice giant.

🔎 Interesting Fact

Uranus rotates with an axial tilt of about 98 degrees, making it appear to rotate almost on its side. This extreme tilt produces very unusual seasonal patterns.
